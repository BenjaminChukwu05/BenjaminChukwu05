Privacy Policy

rclone-personal · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Purpose and scope

rclone-personal is a Google OAuth client configured for the account owner's personal use with rclone on a Fedora Linux workstation. It does not offer accounts or a hosted application to other users.

Google data accessed

After the owner explicitly approves Google authorization, rclone may access Google Drive file and folder metadata, file contents, and permitted file-management operations. The selected full Google Drive OAuth scope allows reading, creating, updating, moving and deleting Drive data, subject to Google permissions and rclone configuration.

How data is used

Access is used only to provide the file-management and backup functions that the owner initiates or configures locally. The OAuth client is not intended for advertising, profiling, selling data, or sharing Google Drive data with unrelated third parties.

Storage and transfer

Rclone stores OAuth credentials and refresh tokens in the owner's local configuration and may cache file content and metadata on the workstation when mounting or transferring files. Google Drive requests are sent to Google's APIs. Local copies or backups remain in the locations the owner configures. The owner is responsible for securing the workstation, configuration, cache and backups.

This GitHub Pages website is informational only. It does not collect Google credentials, receive OAuth tokens, upload Drive contents or store Drive files. As with other websites, the hosting provider may process ordinary connection and access information under its own policies.

Disclosure, retention and deletion

No separate backend database or operator-controlled server is used by this OAuth client. Google Drive data and local cached or copied data remain subject to the owner's chosen retention and deletion practices, the behavior of rclone, and Google's applicable storage and trash policies. Removing local credentials, disconnecting rclone, or revoking Google Account access stops future access until reauthorized; it does not automatically erase files or backups already stored locally.

Access and revocation

The owner can review or revoke the OAuth grant in their Google Account's third-party connections settings. The owner may also remove the rclone remote or local tokens, cache, and retained backups on the workstation as appropriate.

Contact

Questions about this personal integration can be directed through the repository owner's GitHub profile. This application is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, GitHub or the rclone project.
